augments are abilities.

1. Black Powder
	- makes your next two shots deal more damage and travel instantly

2. Electrostatic Dome
	- shield generator. how it works scientifically : generates a static field that detonates projectiles before they damage you.
	
3. HMP Unit
	- Holographic Mech Projection Unit, throws a projector onto the ground that creates a clone of you standing still.
	
4. EMP Engine
	- Shoots a emp charge that disabled enemy augments in a radius near its detonation.
	
5. HRB Cannon
	- Hypervelocity Rod Bundles, basically an air strike on a location you are looking when you click it.

6. Internal Repair Torch
	- repairs all damaged parts 10% over 15 seconds.
